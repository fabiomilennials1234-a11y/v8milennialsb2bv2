# CONTEXT.md — Torque CRM Domain Glossary

Canonical terms used across the system. No implementation details here — this is a glossary.

## Core Entities

- **Relação (Relationship)**: Whether a Lead has ever bought — `Lead` (never) or `Cliente` (at least one won Negócio in its history). Monotonic: a Cliente never reverts to Lead, even after a later loss. Distinct from **Situação**, which is about now.

- **Situação (Standing)**: Whether a Lead has a sale in play right now — `Em negociação` (has an open Negócio; the one shown is the most advanced) or `Sem negócio aberto`. Distinct from **Relação**, which is about history.

  Relação and Situação are read **together and never collapsed into one word**: a returning customer being worked again is `Cliente · Em negociação`, and any single-value status would have to hide one half of that.

- **Leads (page)**: The single source of truth about a Lead's state. A Lead's situation is read there, never inferred from where its Negócios sit in a funnel — the funnel answers "how is this *sale* going", the Leads page answers "who is this person and where do we stand with them".

- **Lead**: A person or company known to the Organization. Has name, company, phone, email, origin, rating (1-5 manual), qualification_score (0-100 auto), qualification_tier, tags, and assigned team members (Pré-vendas/Closer). **A Lead never has a Stage** — it is the durable identity of the person, not a position in a funnel. What occupies a Stage is a **Negócio**. One Lead, many Negócios, over years.

- **Buying-Intent Signal**: A real-time, decaying, evidence-backed hotness score (0-100) assembled from explicit buying signals surfaced during a conversation (pricing/MOQ question, "preciso pra semana que vem", sending a CNPJ/PO, asking lead-time). Distinct from **Qualification Tier**: the tier is a slow lead-quality verdict; the intent signal is "hot RIGHT NOW" and fires real-time actions (handoff, owner notification, cadence change). Both derive from the same extracted signals.

- **Lead Fact Memory**: A durable, agent-curated ledger of stable facts about a Lead (CNPJ, segment, monthly volume, payment terms negotiated, preferred carrier, decision-maker name, known objections) with confidence + provenance + decay. Distinct from **conversation history** (ephemeral, summarized away) and from **Lead 360** (a point-in-time read of CRM columns) — the fact memory persists across conversations and is authored by the agent itself.

- **Cotação (Quote)**: A priced order proposal a Copilot (Vendedor archetype) assembles: line items by SKU + quantity, with MOQ (minimum order quantity) enforcement, a quantity→unit-price tier table, and computed subtotal/IPI/total. Pricing is deterministic (client-configured tiers), never LLM-guessed. Distinct from **Deal** (the broader monetary negotiation record) — a Cotação is one concrete priced offer within it.

- **Qualification Tier**: A first-class enum ranking a Lead's commercial quality: `diamante > ouro > prata > bronze > desqualificado`. Distinct from **Tag** (free-form N:N label, even when a tag happens to be named "Ouro"), from **qualification_score** (0-100 numeric), and from **rating** (1-5 manual human judgment) and **lead_temperature** (hot/warm/cold engagement). Owned by the Qualifier archetype of the Copilot Agent (or set manually). The tier is decided by a deterministic rubric (client-defined thresholds over B2B signals: revenue, purchase volume/recurrence, ICP fit, urgency, region); the LLM only extracts the signals, it does not "judge" the tier.

- **Funnel Health Indicator (Saúde do Funil)**: A manager-facing analytics view of the lead journey as six blocks — Leads → Tratado → Qualificado → Agendado → Compareceu → Vendido — with conversion rates between blocks judged against fixed product benchmarks (>90, >40, >30, >65, >25%) via traffic-light status. Default semantics are **cohort**: the filter is the Lead's *creation* period, and each block counts cohort members that have *ever* reached it (a toggle switches to activity-in-period counting). Blocks are stage-agnostic: Tratado/Qualificado derive from the Effective Tier, Agendado/Compareceu from meeting events, Vendido from a final-positive Orçamentos outcome — so the indicator works unchanged for orgs with customized funnels. Each block drills down by Effective Tier and by **Pré-vendas** (single attribution across all six blocks, including Vendido). Visible to the whole Organization, like Performance.

- **Effective Tier (Tier Efetivo)**: The single Qualification Tier a Lead counts as in metrics when it carries both a pre-qualification and a final qualification: the final qualification always wins (it is the better-informed, post-conversation verdict); the pre-qualification only stands while no final tier exists. A Lead pre-qualified `diamante` then qualified `prata` counts as `prata` — including a final `desqualificado` overriding a positive pre-tier.

- **Lead Tratado (Treated Lead)**: A Lead whose quality has been assessed — it carries an Effective Tier, *any* tier including `desqualificado` (being judged unfit is still treatment; only the absence of any tier means untreated). Funnel-health block 2.

- **Lead Qualificado (Qualified Lead)**: A Lead whose Effective Tier is `prata`, `ouro`, or `diamante`. `bronze` is treated but NOT qualified (too weak to advance); `desqualificado` is treated but not qualified. Funnel-health block 3 — a strict subset of Lead Tratado.

- **Pipeline**: A sequence of stages a **Negócio** moves through (never the Lead — see **Negócio**). There is **one kind of Pipeline** (ADR-0034): every Pipeline is created, renamed, edited and deleted by the user; the ones a new Organization starts with (**Oportunidades** — qualification *and* meeting lifecycle — and **Orçamentos** — closing) are **seeded content**, not a type in the code. The old slugs (`whatsapp`, `confirmacao`, `propostas`) survive only as **aliases** for the seeded Pipelines, for webhooks and API callers that still send them. Behaviour never hangs on a slug — it hangs on explicit configuration (each entry door declares its destination Pipeline; the org's **default Pipeline** is the single fallback) or on a Stage's `stage_role`. A Lead can have Negócios in several Pipelines at once, **and more than one in the same Pipeline** — but each individual Negócio sits in exactly one Pipeline at a time. _Supersedes_ the former invariant "a Lead can exist in multiple pipelines simultaneously": what is in several places is the Lead, only ever through its Negócios. **`pipe_confirmacao` (Agendamentos) is being folded into Oportunidades** — its 6 reminder stages (`confirmar_d5/d3/d2/d1`, `confirmacao_no_dia`) collapse into a card-level Meeting Confirmation Status; only the meeting *outcome* stages survive (Agendado, Remarcar, Compareceu, Não compareceu). Milennials-first rollout. The standalone Agendamentos board is retired.
  > _Changed 2026-09-01 (ADR-0034)._ This entry previously read: *"System pipes: `pipe_whatsapp` (Oportunidades …), `pipe_propostas` (Orçamentos …), `custom_pipelines` (user-defined)"*. That reading made "system vs custom" a type in the product, and the type carried behaviour: custom funnels had no stage dispatch, no Copilot, no analytics, no filters/bulk/pagination and were invisible to the move API — while **34,7% of the 47,7k funnel entries already lived in 79 custom funnels of 45 orgs** (prod, 2026-09-01). Recorded rather than overwritten because the codebase still branches on the type (`pipeline-adapter`'s `type='system'` filter, ~20 RPCs with `p_pipeline_type`, the 5-value CHECK) until the unification waves land.

- **Stage**: A named step within a Pipeline. Dynamic per pipeline via `pipeline_stages`. **A Stage's identity is its UUID** (`pipeline_stages.id`, referenced by `pipeline_entries.stage_id`); `stage_key` is a transitional mirror kept by trigger until the last compat view falls (ADR-0034). **A Stage's meaning is its `stage_role`** (`won`, `lost`, …) — behaviour and metrics read the role, never the name or the key. The seeded Oportunidades track (`novo → abordado → respondeu → esfriou → agendado → remarcar → compareceu → nao_compareceu`) is **seed, not law**: the org can rename, reorder, add or delete any Stage, in any Pipeline, and deleting a Stage that holds cards requires choosing where the N cards move — never deletes a card. In the seeded track, reaching `compareceu` **moves the Negócio into Orçamentos** — it does not create a second one there, so the Oportunidades board holds only work in progress and its `compareceu` column drains as Negócios advance. "How many meetings happened" is answered by **Reunião Realizada** events, never by counting cards sitting in a column. True loss is a loss-reason action, not a stage.
  > _Changed 2026-09-01 (ADR-0034)._ This entry previously presented the linear Oportunidades track as *the* funnel shape and `stage_key` as the identity. The data had already outgrown both: 283 of the 396 active funnels (71%) have no `won` stage at all (ADR-0023 Amendment 1), and text keys collide across funnels the moment stages become fully editable. Recorded rather than overwritten because `stage_key` still has live readers (compat views, older RPCs) until F6 of the unification retires the mirror.

- **Meeting Confirmation Status**: A state on the **Negócio** sitting in the `agendado` stage, tracking whether the booked meeting is confirmed: `pendente → pre_confirmado → confirmado`. Driven by a date-aware button on the kanban card (label is "Pré-confirmar" before the meeting calendar-day, "Confirmar" on the day, in the Organization's timezone). Manual-only in v1 (the Copilot may send reminders but never sets the status). Distinct from a **Stage** (the Negócio stays in `agendado` throughout) and from `is_confirmed` (the legacy boolean this 3-state enum supersedes). Resets to `pendente` when the meeting is rescheduled (Remarcar → new meeting_date).

- **Negócio (Deal)**: One attempt to sell to a Lead — the unit that occupies a Stage and moves through a Pipeline, **any Pipeline**: seeded or user-created, there is only one kind (ADR-0034), and whatever sits in a funnel is a Negócio. Has value (**optional** — a R$ 0 Negócio is normal; a qualification funnel is a funnel of deals that have no value yet), items, and lifecycle (open → closed_won / closed_lost). A Lead can have several, including **two open at once in the same Pipeline** (that is what makes repeat purchase representable); a Negócio belongs to exactly one Lead and inherits its data at creation (**Lead 1 → N Negócio**). **A Negócio occupies exactly one Stage in exactly one Pipeline at a time** — its journey is linear, with a beginning, a middle and an end: when it advances from Oportunidades to Orçamentos it **moves**, it is not copied. Its past is a trail of events, not a trail of cards left behind. **Born when it enters a funnel through an explicit door** — entering a funnel IS the decision that creates it (ADR-0030 Amendment 1): a click, an import, a webhook whose destination someone configured, a Workflow somebody drew and switched on, a scoped API call. What stays forbidden is the *automatic* birth: no Lead becomes a Negócio merely by entering the system. Every Negócio records the door it came through (**Procedência**). Distinct from **Lead** (the person, who never has a Stage) and from **Cotação** (one priced offer *inside* a Negócio). _Avoid_: "card", "oportunidade", "entry" — all three are the same thing seen from the kanban, the sales floor, and the database.
  > _Widened 2026-09-01 (ADR-0034 · ADR-0030 Amendment 1)._ This entry previously separated the card from the Negócio: a card could sit in a funnel with no Negócio behind it, and 26,6% did (12.598 of 47.270, measured 2026-08-28). Now every card is a Negócio and the birth moment is the funnel entry itself. Recorded rather than overwritten because the `backfill_funil_custom` has not run yet and code written before the unification still assumes card ≠ deal (ADR-0031 §2 keys the execution subject on `pipeline_entries.id` for exactly that reason).
  > _Narrowed 2026-08-23 (ADR-0030)._ This entry previously read: *"Born only by human click — no ingest path, no integration, and no automation creates one"*. That reading forbade the very thing the model now needs: a Workflow node that opens a Negócio on a trigger, and a public `POST /deals`. What it was really protecting was *not every Lead that arrives is a sale in progress* — and a Workflow somebody drew, or a scoped API key somebody issued, is a human criterion written down in advance, not a blind automatism. Recorded rather than overwritten because the stricter reading is still what the L1–L4 slice comments assume.
  > _Changed 2026-07-31._ This entry previously read: *"A monetary negotiation attached to a Lead. Distinct from Pipeline — Deal tracks money, Pipeline tracks progress."* That version made the **Lead** the thing that moves, which forbids repeat purchase: the March sale and the September sale would fight over one position. Recorded rather than overwritten because the old reading is still what most of the codebase's older comments assume.

- **Conversation**: A WhatsApp thread between the system and a phone number. Contains Messages. Belongs to an Organization.

- **Message**: A single inbound or outbound communication unit within a Conversation. Types: text, audio, image, video, document, sticker, menu, pix_button.

- **Copilot Agent**: An AI agent that processes inbound WhatsApp messages and generates responses. Canonical model has three **Archetypes**, each with its own Torque-owned, immutable base prompt; the client never edits the base prompt, only fills structured config slots:
  - **Qualificador (Qualifier)**: works inbound NEW/cold ad/WhatsApp leads — qualifies, sets the Qualification Tier, schedules discovery, then hands off the qualified Lead to the Vendedor (via stage move).
  - **Vendedor (Salesperson)**: works qualified Leads — sends proposals/material, negotiates, closes, schedules meetings, sends approved media.
  - **Carteira (Post-sale)**: works existing customers already in the Carteira — drives reorder, upsell, and win-back (resgate) for dormant clients. Reads the Carteira segment (ouro/prata/novo/resgate/dormindo), NOT the Qualification Tier (different scales — must not be conflated).
  An Organization enables any subset, at most one of each archetype. Routing is deterministic by **contact status** (`get_contact_status(phone)` → NOVO / LEAD_NO_PIPELINE → Qualificador; qualified → Vendedor; CLIENTE_CARTEIRA → Carteira) plus the Lead's stage — never complex per-agent rules. (Legacy v1 types — sdr, followup, agendador, prospectador, custom — are deprecated by this three-archetype model.)

- **Human Pause**: A time-bound suspension of a Copilot Agent's responses for a specific Conversation, triggered automatically when a human team member sends a message. The pause resets on each subsequent human message and expires after a configurable duration (default 60 minutes). Distinct from AI Disabled (permanent toggle) and Transfer to Human (agent-initiated state change).

- **Workflow**: A directed acyclic graph (DAG) of automated actions. Triggered by domain events (lead_created, stage_changed, deal_created, deal_won, deal_lost, tag_added, cron). Node types: trigger, action, condition, delay, wait_response, split_ab, copilot, webhook_call, wait_business_window.

- **Sujeito da Execução (Execution Subject)**: What a Workflow execution is *about* — always a Lead, and **also a Negócio** when the trigger came from a funnel. A funnel trigger (`stage_changed`, `deal_won`, `deal_lost`) declares the Negócio; a person trigger (`lead_created`, `tag_added`) declares none, and there the Lead is the right subject. The key is `pipeline_entries.id` — the position that travels — not `deals.id`: **26% of the cards have no row in `deals`** (measured 2026-08-25), so a key on `deals` would blind the engine to most of what enters a Pipeline. `deal_id` rides along when it exists, because it answers value and **Procedência**. An Action about a Pipeline acts on the declared Negócio, and falls back to "the open one, else the most recent" only when none is declared. This is what decides which Negócio receives a **Checklist**, which card `move_stage` moves, and which Stage the `{{estagio}}` variable prints. See ADR-0031.

- **WhatsApp Message Node (Enviar Mensagem)**: A single Workflow action node that can send **any one** WhatsApp (Uazapi) message type, chosen via a **Message Type** selector — collapsing the former four separate nodes (`send_whatsapp`, `send_whatsapp_audio`, `send_whatsapp_image`, and the standalone `generate_ai_message`) into one. It sends **exactly one message per execution** (not a sequence/composer); the config form swaps to match the selected type. Three axes are kept separate: *content* (the chosen Message Type), *channel* (WhatsApp here; **Meta message** and **Meta Template/HSM** stay as their own nodes because the API and capabilities differ), and *delivery* (a node-level **semi-automatic** toggle that routes the message through SDR approval before send instead of auto-sending). The legacy four action types stay alive for already-saved DAGs and are lazily auto-upgraded to the new node on editor load. Backed by a new `send_whatsapp_message` action type carrying a `messageType` discriminator plus that type's config.

- **Message Type**: The single content kind a WhatsApp Message Node sends: `Texto`, `Imagem`, `Vídeo` (MP4 only), `Áudio`, `Sticker`, `Menu` (Uazapi interactive), or `Botão PIX` (Uazapi). `Documento` (PDF/DOC/XLS/PPT/TXT/CSV) is **not** a Message Type — it is its own standalone action node, outside the WhatsApp Message Node. `Texto` has three authoring modes — `Escrever` (free), `Template` (campaign template), and `Gerar com IA` (a prompt generates the message into a variable, default `{{ai_message}}`, which the same node then sends — preserving today's generate-then-send model without a second node). `Áudio` has `Gravar` / `Template`. All text-bearing types resolve **Template Variables** at send time.

- **Campaign**: A time-bound sales initiative parallel to pipelines. Has objective, deadline, AI agent, goals, round robin assignment, and message sequences. A Lead can be enrolled in multiple campaigns.

- **Follow-up**: Umbrella term for a scheduled future re-contact with a Lead. Two distinct kinds that must **not** be conflated:
  - **Copilot Follow-up (Re-engagement Cadence)**: an autonomous WhatsApp re-engagement sequence the Copilot sends when a Lead goes silent. Owned by whichever **Archetype currently owns the Lead** (Qualificador / Vendedor / Carteira) — there is no separate "followup" agent. It fires only from a fixed set of canonical **Follow-up Situations** (never from a generic "last message was ours + N hours" timer). Torque owns the situation set and the default cadence + copy; the Organization only enables/disables each situation and tunes basics (number of touches, spacing, optional copy override). Each touch's text is a Torque-authored base, optionally refined by the LLM from live conversation context (base text is the fallback). A cadence **stops** when any of: the Lead replies, the Lead opts out, steps are exhausted, the owning human replies (cancel — not merely pause), the triggering Situation is resolved (e.g. the proposal reaches `vendido`), or the Lead's owner changes (the old cadence dies; the new owner may start its own).
  - **Follow-up Task**: a human to-do created for a Team Member to contact a Lead — **no message is sent automatically**. Distinct from Copilot Follow-up by actor (human vs Copilot) and output (a task row vs a WhatsApp message). **Scoped like a Checklist** (ADR-0031, extended 2026-08-25): `pipeline_entry_id` set means the task belongs to *that* Negócio; null means it belongs to the person and stands for all their Negócios. Unlike a **Checklist do Negócio**, a task **survives the deletion of its card** (`ON DELETE SET NULL`, not CASCADE) — it has an owner and a due date and sits in somebody's agenda, and "call this person" does not stop making sense because the card is gone. Not to be confused with the legacy pair `source_pipe`/`source_pipe_id`, the un-keyed half-bridge it supersedes.

- **Ação do Dia (Daily Action)**: A one-line to-do a Team Member writes for themself, listed on the Comando dashboard for that day. Scoped exactly like a **Follow-up Task**: born from a funnel card it carries that Negócio, born anywhere else it belongs to the person. Distinct from a **Follow-up Task** (which has a due date, an assignee that may be someone else, and can be created by automation) and from a **Checklist Item** (a step of a named list, not a free line).

- **Follow-up Situation**: One of the fixed, Torque-owned moments that can trigger a Copilot Follow-up, each bound to a funnel position and an owning Archetype, with its own timing, tone, and stop rule. The canonical seven: (Qualificador) **new Lead approached, no reply**; **qualified, no meeting booked**; **cold re-engage** (a Lead parked in a nurture/holding stage that went quiet); (Vendedor) **meeting reminder** (reminder only — the Copilot never sets the Meeting Confirmation Status); **no-show → rebook**; **proposal sent, no reply**; (Carteira) **dormant client → win-back**. A Situation is configured (on/off + basics), never authored from scratch by the Organization. Which of the org's stage_keys map to each Situation is set by `trigger_stage_keys` and populated automatically by an **AI Stage Classifier** (an LLM reads stage names + the `is_final_positive`/`is_final_negative` flags), never hardcoded — see ADR-0006.

- **Custom Field (Campo Personalizado)**: A per-Organization dynamic Lead attribute defined in `lead_custom_fields` (with a `field_type`: text/number/date/select/boolean). Its `field_name` is the human label — often phrased as a form question (e.g. "Qual o tipo do seu negócio?") — and doubles as the field's key. Per-Lead answers live in `lead_custom_field_values`. Referenced in message templates as `{{custom.<field_name>}}` (matched on exact `field_name`). Distinct from the Lead's built-in columns (name, company, …) and from a Tag (a label, not a key/value).

- **Tag**: A free-form, N:N label attached to a Lead (`tags` + `lead_tags`), scoped to an Organization. A Lead can carry several. Distinct from **Qualification Tier** (a ranked enum, even when a tag happens to be named "Ouro") and from a **Custom Field** (a key/value attribute, not a label).

## Post-Sale

- **Procedência (Provenance)**: The door a Negócio was born through — `human` (a click in the interface), `workflow` (an automation node somebody drew and switched on), `api` (a scoped API key), `import` (a spreadsheet), `webhook` (an ingest payload whose destination funnel someone configured — added by ADR-0030 Amendment 1), `backfill` (the 2026-08-23 conversion of every existing card into a Negócio), `backfill_funil_custom` (the funnel-unification backfill: one Negócio per custom card, value null — ADR-0034). Written once at creation and never rewritten: it is a **trail**, not a state — it answers *how did this Negócio come to exist*, a question `created_by` cannot answer because that one names a person and is empty for every door that isn't a person. Distinct from **Origem** (the Lead's marketing source: an ad, a form, a referral) — Procedência is about the Negócio's birth door, Origem is about where the person came from.

- **Carteira (Customer Portfolio)**: The post-sale **facet of a Lead whose Relação is `Cliente`** — health scoring, segmentation (ouro/prata/novo/resgate/dormindo), reorder cycle prediction, churn probability, retention actions. **Not a separate module and not a separate population**: every person the Organization deals with lives in **Leads**, and Carteira is what the Leads page shows once that person has bought. Retires the legacy "Upsell" kanban (`upsell_base` / `upsell_gestao` boards). Distinct from **Pipeline** (Carteira describes a standing relationship by segment; a Pipeline tracks one Negócio's progression).
  > _Changed 2026-07-31 — two corrections in one._
  > **(1) Standalone → facet.** This entry defined Carteira as "a standalone post-sale module, a top-level feature with its own topbar entry". It becomes a facet of the Lead: leads and clients are one population, read in one place. Supersedes **ADR-0005 (carteira-standalone-feature)**, whose premise was the separation. Costless in data: all **739** Carteira clients already point at a Lead — 0 unlinked, 0 orphaned, 0 cross-org (measured 2026-07-31); only 4 sit in the trash.
  > **(2) "Deterministic-by-sale" was false.** The entry claimed an Orçamento reaching `vendido` creates the Carteira Client via the `handle_proposta_vendida` trigger. Measured in production: that function exists with **zero triggers attached and zero callers**, and all 739 rows came from ERP sync. Winning a Negócio does **not** create a Carteira Client today, and making it do so is a new feature with its own cost — not a fix.

- **Order**: A purchase record linked to a Carteira client. Has items, approval workflow, and ERP sync capability.

## Team & Organization

- **Organization (Org)**: A tenant. All data is scoped by `organization_id`. Represents a customer company using Torque CRM.

- **Team Member**: A salesperson within an Organization. Has roles and assignment types (Pré-vendas, Closer). Tracks commissions and goals.

- **Pré-vendas (Pre-Sale Responsible)**: The Team Member accountable for a Lead's pre-sale work — qualification and meeting booking. The ONLY canonical pre-sale assignment on a Lead. Meeting metrics credit the Pré-vendas. Legacy aliases `SDR` (`sdr_id`) and the generic `Responsible` (`responsible_id`) are deprecated and slated for cleanup; during transition they act only as fallback when the canonical field is empty.

- **Closer (Sale Responsible)**: The Team Member accountable for a Lead's sale — proposals, negotiation, closing. The ONLY canonical sale assignment on a Lead. Sales metrics and commissions credit the Closer. Legacy alias `closer_id` is deprecated (fallback-only during transition).

- **Role (code)**: `team_members.role` is the Postgres enum `app_role` = `admin | sdr | closer | agency | bdr | cliente | member`. In production only `admin` and `member` are in use. It is **`member`, never `membro`** — the Portuguese spelling is rejected by the database with `22P02`. **`master` is NOT a role**: it is a separate layer (`is_master_user()`, `useMasterAuth()`) living outside the enum. SDR/Closer exist in the enum but the product treats them as UI labels. The **Gestor de Portfólio** is likewise **not** a value in this enum — it is a separate actor stored outside `team_members` (like the Master), so this invariant is preserved. Mechanical guard: `tests/unit/role-vocabulary.test.ts`.

- **Gestor de Portfólio (Portfolio Manager)**: A cross-org actor **above the Organization and below the Master** — a *scoped Master*. The Master binds a chosen set of Organizations to a Gestor; within each **bound Org** the Gestor operates with **full operational Org-Admin write** (moves cards, edits Leads, changes settings, connects WhatsApp/integrations, fires campaigns) across the whole bound set — with **two carve-outs** below the `admin` ceiling: it does **not** touch the Org's **billing/plan** (a Torque↔client commercial matter, Master-only) and does **not** manage the Org's own **admin/member roster or role permissions** (the client's structure stays the client's/Master's). Everything that is *running the Org's sales operation* it does like an `admin`; everything that is *the Org's contract or org-structure* it cannot. What separates it from the Master: it is **scoped to its bound Orgs, not all Orgs**; it **cannot create Organizations**; and it **cannot reach the Master area**. What separates it from an **Org Admin**: it spans **many** Orgs, is **managed centrally by the Master** (bound/unbound there), and is stored **outside `team_members`** — so it consumes no seat, never appears in an Org's team roster, and is never assignable as a Lead's Pré-vendas/Closer. In place of the Master area it gets its own **Área do Gestor** — an enxuto hub listing its bound Orgs (each a door into that Org, entered like the Master's org-switch) plus the standard Support panel. A Chamado opened by a Gestor is **always anchored to one of its bound Orgs** (never an org-less "gestor-level" ticket — that would fork the Chamado model), carrying an author marker so Torque staff know the author is a Gestor, not a Team Member of that Org. "Parecido com o Master" refers to the *cross-org reach and the scoped-Master architecture*, not to Master-only powers (org creation, Master area).

## Metrics

- **Reunião Marcada (Meeting Booked)**: The countable event of a meeting being scheduled for a Lead. Credits the Lead's **Pré-vendas** in the period the booking happened. Distinct from Reunião Realizada — booking is the pre-sale output metric. **Reschedules do not count as a new booking** (including after a no-show): the same meeting keeps its one `meeting_booked` event with an updated meeting_date. A reschedule only becomes a NEW booking when the new meeting_date differs from the previous one by **more than 30 days**.

- **Reunião Realizada (Meeting Held)**: The countable event of a booked meeting actually happening (`compareceu`). Credits the Lead's **Pré-vendas** in the period of the meeting date. Distinct from Reunião Marcada — held is the quality/outcome metric. Goals can target either kind independently (`reunioes_marcadas` / `reunioes_realizadas` are separate goal types; the legacy single `reunioes` goal type measured held only).

- **Produtividade (Activity-in-Period Indicator)**: An Organization-wide block on the Performance page counting **what was *done* in a date range** — four counts: **Novos leads**, **Reuniões Marcadas**, **Reuniões Realizadas** (Comparecidas), and **Vendido**. Its defining trait: every count is keyed to the **date of the action itself** (the event's occurrence — booking date for Marcada, meeting date for Comparecida, sale date for Vendido, creation date for Novos leads), **never** to the Lead's creation/entry date. A Lead that entered in May whose meeting was booked in June counts in June — closing the gap where creation-cohort metrics silently drop cross-period activity. Filterable by a **free date range** (with presets) and optionally narrowed to a single **Team Member** (attribution = **Pré-vendas** for meetings, **Closer** for sales). Each count drills down to a per-Lead list with the exact action timestamp. Visible to the whole Organization, like the rest of Performance. Distinct from the **Funnel Health Indicator**: that view defaults to **cohort-by-creation** with conversion rates + traffic-light benchmarks for a manager audience, while Produtividade is **raw counts by action-date with per-Lead drill** for the whole team. (Funnel Health's activity-in-period toggle shares the date semantics but not the audience or the shape.)

## Communication

- **Message Gateway**: Single entry point for all outbound WhatsApp messages. Handles: instance resolution, phone normalization, dedup, rate limiting, provider dispatch, persistence, and structured logging.

- **Instance (WhatsApp)**: A WhatsApp phone number connection. Two kinds, and the difference is a domain difference, not a vendor detail: a **Chip** (an ordinary WhatsApp account driven by the Uazapi provider) and an **Official Channel** (a WhatsApp Business Platform number reached through NotificaMe). An Organization can have multiple instances of either kind.
- **Conversa do Lead**: The (Lead ↔ inbox) pair that actually carries history. An Organization has many inboxes — WhatsApp Instances plus social channels — and one Lead may hold a Conversa do Lead in several of them at once (measured in production: one phone with three). Distinct from **Conversation**, which is the Copilot runtime thread between a deployed Agent and a Lead (`conversations` + `conversation_messages`), and from `whatsapp_conversations`, which is only an archive/delete marker table. This term is what the inbox selector lists; before it was named, every card invented its own idea of "the lead's conversation" and they disagreed.
- **Resolução de Instância (Instance Resolution)**: Deciding which inbox to speak through. It has **two implementations that are allowed to differ, on purpose**: the **Message Gateway** resolves automatically in order to *send*; the UI resolves *assisted* — asking the user — in order to *open*. Naming the operation once forces that divergence to be documented instead of drifting: for years only the backend side had a name, and the frontend reimplemented it silently with a different rule.

- **Conversation Thread**: The 1:1 message history between an Organization and a phone number, carried by an Instance. Group messages are not part of it. The Instance of the **most recent** message — in **either** direction — is the thread's **live Instance**: an outbound message opens a thread just as an inbound one does, so a Workflow's first message node anchors the thread and the later nodes inherit it. Read by **normalized phone + Organization**, never by `lead_id` (52% of 1:1 messages carry no `lead_id`).

- **Instance Routing Policy**: The rule a **WhatsApp Message Node** declares to pick its outbound **Instance**, replacing the former "Automático (primeira disponível)" — which resolved to an unordered row and so sent from a random connected number. Three policies: `conversation` (the **Conversation Thread**'s live Instance; the default), `responsible` (the Instance bound to the Lead's responsible team member), and `fixed` (an Instance named on the node). Policies `conversation` and `responsible` carry an explicit **fallback Instance** on the node, used when the Lead has no thread — the node states both legs of the rule on one screen. An Organization with exactly **one** connected Instance always uses it, ahead of any failure: there is no wrong choice where there is no choice. A resolved Instance that is disconnected **fails the node** with its own error code — never a silent switch to another number. Scoped to Workflow message nodes; other outbound paths are unchanged. See ADR-0025.

- **Mass Send (Disparo)**: A one-time bulk outbound broadcast to many recipients through a single Instance. Sourced from CSV paste or a lead selection. Tracked as a job with progress counters and pause/resume/stop controls. Distinct from Campaign (no stages, no enrollment, no rules) and Workflow (not event-triggered). **The chosen Instance decides the regime**: on a Chip the content is free text; on an Official Channel the content is necessarily an approved Template, because the recipients of a broadcast are by definition outside the 24-hour window, and the failure the guardrails exist to prevent is not a banned chip but a downgraded account (messaging tier and quality rating). Audience, Daily Blast Budget, Blast Plan and the audience refinements are the same in both regimes — only content and guardrails differ.

- **Quick Blast (Disparo Rápido)**: An ad-hoc Mass Send triggered directly from a kanban/list lead selection — "de supetão", without planning. Reuses the Mass Send dispatch core. Defining traits: no role gate (any logged-in member may fire it, scoped to their Organization by RLS), with an Organization-level cap on leads-per-blast as the safety guardrail instead of a permission; per-recipient personalization (variables + spintax) to reduce ban risk; optional single image; randomized inter-message delay. A Quick Blast is a Mass Send — same domain concept, different entry point and access policy.

- **Blast Audience**: The resolved set of Leads a Mass Send targets. Selected from a source — all Leads in a Stage, the Leads matching the board's active filter, or a manual card selection — then optionally narrowed by two refinements: **contact recency** (exclude Leads who already received a blast within a configurable window, default 7 days) and **reply status** ("não respondeu" = a Lead with zero inbound Messages after their most recent blast send). These refinements supersede the manual "Reencaminhar disparo" stage workaround some Orgs created to re-target non-responders by hand.

- **Daily Blast Budget**: An Organization-wide ceiling on how many Leads may be messaged via Mass Send per calendar day, summed across every blast — manual Quick Blasts and Blast Plan lots alike (default 200). Server-enforced, fail-closed. Supersedes the per-blast cap framing of ADR-0002: the guardrail is now throughput-per-day, not size-per-blast. A blast or lot that would exceed the remaining budget is truncated or deferred to the next day.

- **Spend Cap (Teto de Gasto)**: A money ceiling on a Mass Send sent through an Official Channel, where every delivered template message is billed by Meta and the price depends on the template's category. Distinct from the **Daily Blast Budget**, which counts *people per day* and exists to protect a Chip from being banned: a Spend Cap counts *money* and exists to protect the Organization from a bill it did not intend. The two coexist and measure different things. A Spend Cap is the guardrail *instead of* a category allowlist, because a template's category is set by Meta and may be reclassified — a Template approved as utility today can be billed as marketing tomorrow without anyone editing it, so a rule phrased in categories cannot bound spend.

- **Blast Recipient**: One person's place in a Mass Send, carrying its own state (`pending → sent → delivered | failed`), its own failure reason as the channel reported it, and — on an Official Channel — its own cost. It is the unit of resumption: pausing means the worker stops claiming recipients, and resuming continues at the next pending one. On an Official Channel `sent` and `delivered` are genuinely different facts and both matter, because Meta bills on **delivery** and a message may be delivered long after the send finished, or silently dropped when its TTL expires — so a recipient that is never confirmed ends in an explicit *unconfirmed* state rather than being counted either way.

- **Suppression List**: The set of people an Organization must not include in a Mass Send, regardless of Audience. Fed by three producers: the channel reporting that the person opted out of marketing, an inbound message asking to stop, and a manual mark by a human. Distinct from the Audience refinements (which are about *timing* — who was contacted recently, who has not replied): suppression is about *permission*, and it outlives any single blast. Consent and its revocation are recorded with their source and moment, so the reason a person is suppressed is auditable.

- **Blast Plan**: A Mass Send whose Audience exceeds one day's budget, sliced into daily lots over consecutive days. The Audience is **frozen at creation** (a snapshot — Leads that enter the source Stage afterward are not added); each lot is released by a daily job that re-applies the audience refinements (reply status, contact recency) at send time and consumes at most the remaining Daily Blast Budget. A Blast Plan is finite and self-terminating — distinct from a Workflow (event-triggered, standing) and a Campaign (stages + enrollment). It is "a blast spread over days," not a rule.

- **Voice Session**: An Organization's WhatsApp number paired for **voice**, held by the TorqueCalls VPS. Distinct from an **Instance**, which carries messages — though a Voice Session is bound to one. Chosen per Organization, not per operator: every seller dials through the same Voice Session, so the ordering counter the VPS keeps is shared across all their concurrent calls. Reaches `open` only through a signed event from the VPS. See ADR-0024.

- **Voice Call**: One outbound dial from an operator to a Lead over WhatsApp, carried by a **Voice Session**. Authorized before it rings — the CRM mints the call id, and the VPS adopts it. Who may dial through a given number follows the **same rule as the message inbox**: a number with nobody on its allowed-members list is open to the whole Organization; a number with a list is restricted to it; admins and masters bypass.

- **Call Log**: The Lead-facing record of a call — outcome, duration, direction — projected automatically from the Voice Call when it ends. Duration counts from **answer**, not from dial: time spent ringing is not conversation. A call nobody answered still produces a Call Log, because "they didn't pick up" is information; it produces no **Call Recording**, because there is no conversation to hear.

- **Inbound Voice Call**: A **Voice Call** the customer places to the Organization's number. Rings for whoever holds access to the **Instance** — the same allowed-members list the message inbox uses, an empty list meaning the whole Organization — which is the Instance gate of ADR-0025 answering the opposite question: on the way out it asks *may this seller use this number?*, on the way in it answers *who should be rung?*. That inversion is what makes it work when there is **no operator yet**. The CRM rings while WhatsApp keeps the call alive and **never rejects the offer itself**: it gives up ringing when WhatsApp gives up, so a CRM timer never decides for whoever is holding the phone. The paired handset rings alongside — WhatsApp settles the race, first to answer takes it. Whoever answers **becomes the operator**, and from that follow the recording gate and the attributed history. A caller with no Lead is still recorded, tied by phone (47% of contacts carry no Lead); voice switched off silences the ring but **still records that the call came in**, because the customer calling is a fact of the relationship, not of the feature. See ADR-0027.

- **Call Recording**: The audio of an answered **Voice Call**, captured by the VPS — never by the operator's browser, so the sample never depends on someone keeping a tab open. **Stereo by construction**: the operator on one channel, the Lead on the other, because the two legs arrive separately and merging them would discard information already in hand — speaking share, interruptions, and later a transcript that knows the speaker without guessing. Lives **90 days**, then the object itself is deleted, not merely its reference. Admins and gestores hear every recording in the Organization; a seller hears **only their own** — which is what makes it self-correction rather than surveillance, and is a deliberate departure from the Lead-scoped visibility rule, since training material belongs to whoever produced it. Recorded **without notice to the Lead** by CTO decision, and each recording carries the regime it was born under so the choice stays reversible going forward. See ADR-0026.

## Automation

- **Workflow Execution**: One passage of a Lead through a Workflow. Carries the pointer to the node it is stopped at and survives restarts and deploys — a Workflow spanning days exists only as this persisted row, never as a live process. Distinct from the Workflow (the DAG definition, shared by all Leads) as an instance is from its class.

- **Due**: The instant a Workflow Execution becomes eligible to run again (`next_run_at`). Distinct from **Started** (when the Execution was born) and from **Claimed** (when a worker took it). These three drift far apart: an Execution parked in a 7-day delay is Started long before it is Due. Ordering the queue by Started rather than Due is the classic error — it measures age, not need.

- **Wait**: A Workflow Execution sleeping inside a delay or wait_response node, by design. **A Wait is not lateness.** Its duration is authored by the Organization and carries no signal about system health.

- **Lag**: The elapsed time between **Due** and **Claimed** — how long an Execution that was ready to run sat unattended. **This is the only measure of customer-visible pain in the automation engine**, and the only one a Team Member perceives as "the automation didn't fire". Deliberately distinct from Wait (authored, healthy) and from total Execution duration (dominated by authored delays).

- **Claim**: The act of a worker taking ownership of a batch of Due Executions, marking them so no other worker takes the same ones. A claim that is never released — because the worker died — must be reclaimable by timeout, or the Executions are stranded.


- **Action Handler**: A function that executes a specific domain operation (move_stage, send_whatsapp, update_lead, etc.). Registered in a handler map and dispatched by the Workflow engine or Copilot.

- **Template Variable**: A `{{...}}` placeholder in an outbound message template, resolved per-Lead at send time by the Workflow message resolver. Three families: **built-in** (`{{nome}}`, `{{empresa}}`, `{{ai_resumo}}`, …) mapped to Lead/Pipeline/AI attributes; **Custom Field** (`{{custom.<field_name>}}`) → the Lead's answer for that field, empty if unanswered; **Tag** (`{{tag.<tag_name>}}`) → the tag name itself **if the Lead carries that tag, else empty** (a conditional echo, not a value lookup). All unresolved/absent values render as empty string (no fallback). The authoring UI offers a picker that lists the Organization's real Custom Fields and Tags so the author clicks instead of typing the key.

- **Dead Letter Event**: A domain event that was emitted but had no handler, or whose handler failed/timed out. Stored for audit and manual resolution.

## Avisos

- **Aviso (Notification)**: A living row addressed to one Team Member inside one Organization, born from a single domain event and **mutable until read**. Unlike an event, it absorbs its successors: the second, third and tenth message of the same conversation raise its counter instead of creating siblings. Read closes it forever — a later event of the same kind starts a new Aviso. Every Aviso is recorded regardless of the recipient's preferences; preferences govern **delivery** (sound, toast, push), never the record. _Avoid_: notificação, alerta, alert.

- **Group Key**: The identity an Aviso coalesces on — `msg:<lead_id>` for a conversation, `wf:<workflow_id>` for a failing automation, `fup:<id>:<date>` for a materialized daily reminder. It is what "the same thing happening again" means for that kind of Aviso, and it is deliberately not the entity the Aviso opens: a failing automation groups by Workflow but opens on the Execution. Unique per recipient **only while unread**.

- **Dono do Aviso (Recipient)**: The single person an Aviso is addressed to. Derived, never chosen: for anything Lead-shaped it is the **Closer**, falling back to the **Pré-vendas** (canonical fields only; the legacy aliases serve as fallback exactly as the Lead's own definition allows); for an automation failure it is every `admin` of the Organization. Because assignment names a **Team Member** while an Aviso is addressed to a login, the derivation ends at that member's user account — **a Team Member with no user account cannot be a Dono**, and neither can an inactive one. **A Lead with no owner produces no Aviso** — absent ownership is an assignment problem, and broadcasting to everyone converts it into noise for everyone.

- **Canal Quente / Canal Frio (Hot / Cold Channel)**: The two delivery surfaces of an Aviso. **Quente** = toast plus sound, reserved for what changes if you learn it now rather than in forty minutes (a blocked automation, a message from your Lead, a Lead just assigned to you). **Frio** = the bell badge, for everything true but not urgent. The split is temporal, not by importance: a meeting booked for tomorrow is important and cold.

## Intelligence

- **Oraculo Comercial**: A specialized Copilot variant that provides sales coaching and metric analysis. Not a separate domain — it's a Copilot agent type.

- **Copilot Builder**: An internal AI assistant that interviews the person creating a Copilot Agent and progressively fills the agent's configuration (prompt sections, tools, instructions, funnel wiring, knowledge). It builds an Agent; it is not itself a deployed Agent and never talks to Leads. Distinct from the Copilot Agent it produces.

- **Builder Session**: The persistent, re-openable conversation between a user and the Copilot Builder for a given Agent. Survives across visits — the user can return to review the interview history and continue revising the Agent with the Builder. Distinct from a (runtime) Conversation, which is between a deployed Agent and a Lead.

## Engagement

- **Checklist**: A template-driven task list attached to a **Negócio** or to a **Lead**. A **template** Checklist (`lead_id` null) is the reusable definition; applying it **copies** it — an instance with its own items. Two scopes, and the difference is the whole point:
  - **Checklist do Negócio** (`pipeline_entry_id` set) — the work of *this* sale. Born from a funnel event: the Stage that names a template, the `apply_checklist` Workflow action inside a funnel-triggered Workflow, or a click inside the Negócio. Idempotent per **(Negócio, template)**, so two Negócios of the same Lead each get their own copy.
  - **Checklist da Pessoa** (`pipeline_entry_id` null) — the work about *the person*, valid across all their Negócios. Born from a person event (a Tag, a Lead created) or applied from the Lead's own screen. Idempotent per **(Lead, template)**, counted only among the Checklists that have no Negócio.

  Both carry `lead_id`: a Checklist do Negócio is still about the person behind it, and the Leads page must not lose sight of what the automation applied. A Negócio never shows the Checklists of a *sibling* Negócio — the card states how many exist elsewhere, without listing them. Completion is tracked per item.

  > _Changed 2026-08-25 (ADR-0031)._ This entry previously read: *"A template-driven task list attached to a Lead … A Lead may hold many Checklists at once"*. That reading made a Checklist a property of the person, so the **second** Negócio of a Lead silently received nothing — `ON CONFLICT (lead_id, source_template_id) DO NOTHING` had already been satisfied by the first. Measured before the change: **146 of the 759** template-applied Checklists sat on Leads with 2+ Negócios. Recorded rather than overwritten because the 1.338 Checklists that predate the change are all person-scoped, and they are meant to stay that way — claiming they belong to one Negócio would invent a fact.

- **Checklist Item**: One task line on a Checklist, with a completed/not-completed state. An applied item is a **copy** of a template item and carries **lineage** back to it (`template_item_id`) — the stable identity that lets a Workflow address "this specific item" across the template→instance copy, since the copy's own id is minted only at apply time. Renaming or reordering an item does not break the lineage. An item is completed **inside its own Checklist**: ticking an item on a Checklist do Negócio never ticks anything on a sibling Negócio, while ticking one on a Checklist da Pessoa shows as ticked everywhere, because it is one row. Distinct from an **Activity** (an item is a task to do; an Activity is the audit record of a thing already done).

- **Activity**: An audit log entry recording any domain action (call logged, email sent, stage moved, etc.). Consumer-only — records events from all modules.

- **Gamification**: Badges, awards, competitions, streaks, and milestones for sales team motivation.

## Billing

Este é o dinheiro que a **Organização deve ao Torque** — direção oposta à de `Order`, `Cotação` e `Título`, que descrevem o dinheiro que o cliente final deve à Organização. Quando houver risco de leitura dupla, qualificar ("cobrança no Asaas").

- **Subscription Plan**: A pricing tier for an Organization. Managed via Asaas payment gateway. Controls feature access via quotas and feature flags.

- **Contratação**: Um pacote de venda do Torque congelado e precificado, esperando pagamento. Carrega plano de partida, features, limites, número de assentos, ciclo, meio de pagamento e preço. Nasce de duas origens — montada por um Master (venda assistida, entregue como link com token) ou escolhida pelo próprio cliente no site (self-serve, resolvida por sessão) — e as duas viram a mesma coisa. _Avoid_: **Pedido** e **Cotação**, que no glossário descrevem a venda da Organização para o cliente final dela, não a venda do Torque para a Organização.

- **Comprador (Buyer)**: A pessoa jurídica ou física que paga uma Contratação — razão social, documento fiscal (CPF ou CNPJ) e e-mail fiscal. Distinto do **usuário** que se cadastrou e da **Organização** que será provisionada: quem opera o produto e quem paga a conta não são necessariamente a mesma entidade. Trocar o documento fiscal cria um cliente novo no gateway, e o meio de pagamento salvo não atravessa.

- **Snapshot da Assinatura**: A cópia congelada da Contratação gravada no instante do pagamento, e a camada BASE de onde o produto lê features e limites. É o contrato: mudança posterior no catálogo não alcança quem já comprou — é daqui que sai o grandfathering. Append-only. Distinto do **Subscription Plan** (o catálogo, mutável) e de uma concessão temporária de feature (que tem validade).

- **Provisionamento**: O trabalho de transformar "pagou" em "tem acesso" — criar ou atualizar a Organização, criar o admin, gravar plano e validade, ligar o Snapshot. Roda **fora** do webhook do gateway, em fila própria, porque falhar dentro do webhook deixaria a Organização paga e inacessível em silêncio.

- **Cota (Quota)**: O teto de um recurso para uma Organização (assentos, instâncias de WhatsApp, agentes de Copilot). É **soma**, não disputa: base do plano + o que foi comprado a mais + ajuste manual do Master. O valor efetivo é derivado e não aceita escrita direta. Distinto de **feature**, que é sobrescrita — a última camada a falar vence.

## Marketing

- **Lead Form**: An external capture form that feeds Leads into the system via `lead-webhook`. A **Meta Lead Form** is the native Lead Ads form, owned by a **Page** (not an Ad Account). Its submissions are pulled into Torque by **scheduled polling** of the Graph API (`/{form}/leads`, ~5 min cron) using the Torque Meta System User token — not a realtime webhook and no `lead-webhook` / Make hop. Polling was chosen over push because it needs zero per-app webhook configuration and fits the System-User-token + master-binding model; the cost is ~5 min latency, deemed irrelevant for B2B follow-up.

- **UTM**: Tracking parameters (source, medium, campaign) attached to Lead origin for marketing attribution.

- **Torque Meta System User**: The single, long-lived Meta System User token belonging to Torque's own Business Manager. Clients grant Torque **partner access** to their Meta assets (Pages + Ad Accounts); the System User token then reads/acts across all granted assets with one credential. Replaces the legacy **per-org OAuth** model (`meta_connections` / `meta_pages`, user-login-scoped tokens that expire ~60 days). A platform-level secret (service-role only), never per-org. Distinct from a Page Access Token (one page) and from an OAuth user token (one logged-in user).

- **Lead Conversion Signal (Meta)**: A CRM funnel event sent **back** to Meta (via the Conversions API, `action_source: system_generated`) keyed by the Lead's stored Meta lead id (`leadgen_id`), so Meta's **Conversion Leads** campaign optimization learns which ad-sourced Leads advance. Escalated (multiple distinct `event_name`s as the Lead progresses — e.g. qualified, meeting, sold), **never carrying a monetary value** (revenue is not exposed to Meta, by policy). Only fires for Meta-native-form Leads (those with a `leadgen_id` join key); Leads without one (e.g. click-to-WhatsApp ads, no form) cannot be signalled. Idempotent — each `event_name` is sent at most once per Lead. Requires the client's campaign to be configured for Conversion Leads optimization on the Meta side, else the event lands but does not optimize. Distinct from **meta-ads-insights** (reads ad metrics *in*; this writes Lead outcomes *out*).

- **Meta Asset Binding**: A master-managed link between an **Organization** and the Meta assets it owns — one or more **Pages** and one or more **Ad Accounts** (`act_…`). Set manually in the Master area by selecting, per Org, which assets (enumerated from the Torque Meta System User token) belong to it. Two purposes: a **Page** binding routes inbound Meta Lead Forms and messages to the Org; an **Ad Account** binding enables reading insights and sending conversion signals back (campaign optimization). A Page maps to exactly one Org (no fan-out). Supersedes the OAuth-populated `meta_pages.organization_id` as the source of truth for which Org a Meta asset feeds.

## Suporte (Torque → Organização)

- **Chamado (Support Ticket)**: A request for help raised by a user of an Organization **to Torque's own support staff** — a bug report, a question, or a request. Direction is what defines it: it flows *up* from a tenant to the vendor, never between the Organization and its Leads. A Chamado carries a mutable **`tipo`** (`bug` / `duvida` / `solicitacao`) — the type is the *output of triage*, not a fact known at open time (a "bug" that turns out to be a misconfigured permission stays the same Chamado and changes type). It also carries a status, a severity, and an immutable **Support Context** snapshot. Distinct from a **Conversation** (a WhatsApp thread with a Lead's phone number), from a **Follow-up Task** (a human to-do about a Lead), and from a **Help Article** (static, one-way documentation — the deflection layer a Chamado is opened *after*).

- **Comentário (Ticket Comment)**: One entry in a Chamado's thread, authored by either the Organization's user or by Torque support staff. Carries **`is_internal`**: an internal comment is a staff-only note, never visible to the Organization. Deliberately **not** called a Message — **Message** is reserved for a WhatsApp/Meta communication unit inside a Conversation, and conflating the two would poison both the Copilot's context loaders and any semantic search over the corpus.

- **Anexo (Attachment)**: A file sent as evidence on a Chamado — by the Organization's user or by Torque staff, in either direction. It belongs to a **Comentário**, not to the Chamado: what the two parties exchange is a conversation, and evidence torn from its turn and stacked at the foot of the thread loses both its author and its moment. The one exception is the Anexo sent **when the Chamado is opened**, which has no Comentário to belong to and hangs off the Chamado itself — the same standing as the **Support Context**, and captured at the same instant. An Anexo is **immutable and staff-deleted only**: it is evidence, and evidence the other party has already seen is not rewritten. It is retained 90 days past `fechado` and then destroyed while the thread survives — the file carries the tenant's Leads' PII, and the purpose that justified holding it ends when the Chamado does. Distinct from **Mídia do Artigo** (Help Center imagery — public, permanent, authored by Torque) and from a WhatsApp media **Message** (which flows *outward* to a Lead).

- **Anexo Interno (Internal Attachment)**: An Anexo on a staff-only Comentário — invisible to the Organization, exactly as the note it accompanies. It is named separately because visibility is carried by the **storage path** (`internal/`), not by the row: authorization for a file is derived from its path *before* any table is read, so an internal Anexo written to the ordinary path would leak while its note stayed hidden. This is also why **`is_internal` is immutable** — a note that changed visibility after its file was written would put the path and the database in silent disagreement.

- **Support Context**: The immutable snapshot captured at the moment a Chamado is opened — the technical evidence the user cannot be asked to reproduce by hand (current route, app version, browser, the last client-side errors, and the `session_id` that ties the Chamado to the backend work recorded in `runtime_logs`). Distinct from the Chamado's mutable fields: the context describes *the world when it broke*, and is never edited afterwards.

- **Impacto**: What the Organization's user states, at open time, about being blocked — *"estou parado" / "consigo contornar" / "é um incômodo"*. It is a **fact the user knows**. Deliberately **not** Severidade: a user cannot know whether a defect is critical, because criticality depends on how many other Organizations are hit — information only Torque holds. Asking the user for severity produces a queue where everything is critical.

- **Severidade**: Torque support staff's verdict on a Chamado's urgency (`baixa` / `media` / `alta` / `critica`), **derived** from the Impacto plus cross-tenant knowledge. Written only by staff, never by the Organization.

- **Chamado Lifecycle**: `aberto → em_andamento → aguardando_cliente → resolvido → fechado`. `aberto` *means* untriaged — there is no separate triaged state. `aguardando_cliente` is the state in which response-time accounting stops, so Torque is not measured against a silent customer. `resolvido` is staff's claim of a fix; `fechado` is terminal and arrives automatically 7 days later if the Organization does not reopen. **Reopening is not a state**: it returns the Chamado to `aberto` and increments a reopen count — a Chamado reopened three times is evidence the fix never landed, and that signal must accumulate rather than be overwritten.

- **Dono do Chamado (Owner)**: The one member of Torque's support staff currently working a Chamado. Held by **claim** ("Pegar") rather than dispatch — notification goes to the whole support group, and whoever takes it marks themselves. Its purpose is to stop two people answering the same customer, not to model a work queue. Reassignment ("Passar para…") is permitted. Always a staff member, never a Team Member of the Organization (different populations).

- **Meta de Primeira Resposta (First-Response Target)**: A **policy** — a duration per Severidade (`critica` 1h, `alta` 4h, `media` 1 útil, `baixa` 3 úteis) — deliberately **not an SLA**: nothing is promised to any Organization, and no contract references it. It lives in one place as a constant, never copied onto individual Chamados; a Chamado's deadline is *derived* from its Severidade, its open time, and the time it spent in `aguardando_cliente`. Freezing the target onto each row would mean that changing the policy either rewrites history or silently leaves two policies in force.

- **Defeito (Defect)**: The underlying platform bug that several Chamados across different Organizations may each be a report of. It is **not modelled in this database** — the defect tracker is GitHub Issues, and each affected Chamado carries the issue's URL. This is what makes Severidade honest: counting Chamados grouped by defect URL tells staff how many Organizations a bug actually hits, which is precisely the knowledge a single Organization's user cannot have. A Defeito belongs to no tenant.

## Central de Ajuda (Deflexão)

- **Central de Ajuda (Help Center)**: The **deflection layer** — a body of static, one-way documentation a user searches *before* opening a Chamado, surfaced route-aware inside the support panel. One-way is what separates it from a Chamado: it never replies. Articles belong either to Torque (global, shared by every Organization) or to a single Organization (its own).

- **Artigo de Ajuda (Help Article)**: One piece of that documentation — a title, a body, optional images, and **at most one** embedded Vídeo do Artigo. Static and one-way, distinct from a Chamado (a two-way request that flows *up* to the vendor). Route-tagged so it surfaces on the very screen it explains — an article tagged `funis` appears as "sugerido" to anyone on `/funis`.

- **Vídeo do Artigo (Article Video)**: A single walkthrough video shown at the top of a Help Article, referenced **by URL from an allowlisted host** (Loom or YouTube) — never a file the tenant uploads, never arbitrary embed markup. Its mere presence is what the **"▶ vídeo"** badge announces in the article list. An article has zero or one; a gallery of videos is deliberately not a concept.

- **Feedback do Artigo ("Foi útil?")**: A reader's single, changeable 👍/👎 verdict on a Help Article, with an **optional reason** offered only on 👎. Aggregated **anonymously** for whoever owns the article (Torque for global articles, the Organization's admin for its own) — a signal to *improve the article*, never a record of who complained. Distinct from a Chamado: it is one-way sentiment about documentation, not a request that anyone answers.

## Onboarding & Setup

- **Comece aqui (Setup Hub)**: A guided, revisitable onboarding hub at `/comecar` that teaches an Organization how to configure each system area in plain language and routes into the raw config screens to do it. **Distinct from Pitstop** (the `/configuracoes` Settings page — the raw config engine with tabs like WhatsApp, templates, API keys): the hub *teaches and points*, Pitstop *executes*. The hub never duplicates a Settings control; its action buttons deep-link into the right Pitstop tab or feature screen. Unlike the legacy one-time **Onboarding Wizard**, the hub is permanent and can be reopened any time to configure more.

- **Setup Area**: One configurable surface shown as a card in the Setup Hub. Each area has plain-language teaching (**O que é** / **Por que importa** / **Como configurar**) plus a mini-checklist of **Setup Steps**. Areas are split into **Essencial** (shown to every Org — Conectar WhatsApp, Funis, Combustível, Copilot/Agente IA, Pilotos/Equipe) and **Conforme seu uso** (shown only when the Org's plan **feature-flag** is on — e.g. Mensagens Meta, Automações, Disparos, Carteira, Metas/Comissões, Produtos, Templates, Agenda/TV). A flag that is off hides the area entirely.

- **Setup Step**: A single sub-task inside a Setup Area, marked done by **live detection of real data** (e.g. "WhatsApp connected" = an instance exists; "Has a funnel" = ≥1 pipeline; "Agent active" = a copilot agent is enabled) — never a manual checkbox. An area's completion **percentage is honest count-based**: detected steps ÷ total steps, not an arbitrary weighting.

- **Aha-moment (CRM)**: The point a new Org first feels the product's value — defined as the **AI agent (Copilot) holding/qualifying a real Lead conversation**. The Essencial setup spine is ordered to reach it fast (channel → funnel → fuel → agent). Drawn from the studied insight that the best onboarding sells the *outcome* and reaches value quickly, not lists features.

- **Onboarding Wizard** *(existing, legacy-adjacent)*: The one-time, full-screen first-run flow (`OnboardingWizard`) gated by `OnboardingGate`: a profile **quiz** (segment, ticket, cycle, team, SDR…) that auto-applies suggested pipelines, then team/WhatsApp/lead activation. Its quiz answers are reused by the Setup Hub's personalized hero ("Pro seu perfil … 3 funis + Bia qualificando 24/7"); an Org that skipped the quiz gets a neutral hero instead.

- **Primeiros passos (Checklist pill)**: The persistent, dismissible 6-step pill (`OnboardingChecklist`, Vercel/Linear style) that lives in the app header after the wizard. It is the **minimized form of the Setup Hub** — same progress source of truth — and expanding it / "ver tudo" opens `/comecar`. Not a separate progress model.
